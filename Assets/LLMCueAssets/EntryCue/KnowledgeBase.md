## Overview

Aurora Live is a live-streamed concert experienced in virtual reality. The user is currently in their real room (AR passthrough); the transition takes them into the virtual venue where the show is happening now. Entering is free of charge and can be done at any moment while the event runs.

## Environment

The venue is a floating open-air stage under a night sky with auroras. The user stands on a glowing viewing platform about 20 meters from the stage. There are three viewpoints: front row, elevated side balcony, and a calm zone further back with fewer people and quieter audio. The space is large and open; there is no danger of anything coming close to the user's face.

## Controls and interaction

- Look around freely by turning the head; no artificial rotation.
- A laser pointer from the right controller selects things; the A button confirms, B goes back.
- Movement is by teleport between the three viewpoints only — no smooth locomotion, so motion sickness risk is very low.
- The Meta button always opens the system menu; from there "Leave event" returns to the real room immediately.

## Audio

Music is live spatial audio coming from the stage direction. Crowd sounds come from around the user. A volume slider is on the left wrist menu. In the calm zone, crowd audio is reduced to a murmur.

## Social situation

Other visitors are present as simple avatars with name tags. Nearby visitors can be heard talking; the user's own microphone is MUTED by default and only transmits after explicitly unmuting on the wrist menu. Friends can be joined via invite, but the user can also attend fully anonymously. Nobody can touch, block, or follow the user; a personal space bubble is always on.

## The transition itself

- Entry: after confirming, the real room fades out and the venue fades in over about 5 seconds. The user stays standing or seated exactly as they are; no movement is required during the transition.
- During the fade, the music becomes audible first, so the user hears where they are going before they see it.
- Exit: hold the Meta button or choose "Leave event" — the venue fades out and the real room returns in about 3 seconds. Nothing in the real room changes while the user is away.
- The user can re-enter and leave as often as they like.

## What changes for the user

- Vision: the real room becomes fully invisible while in VR (no passthrough during the event).
- The physical space needed is just standing or sitting in place; no walking is required in VR.
- Time: the event is live; leaving and re-entering means missing the moments in between.
- Comfort: if anything feels overwhelming, the calm zone or leaving is always one action away.
