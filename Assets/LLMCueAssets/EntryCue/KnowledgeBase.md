# Target context: a VR representation of the chemistry lab

This is the knowledge the cue may draw on. Answer ONLY from what is written here.
(Written in English; if the user speaks German, answer in German.)

## The situation

The user is walking through the building in AR, on the way to a lecture room. A message just arrived from Peter, asking how many working stations there are in the chemistry lab. As the user walks a few meters further, an entry cue appears to their right — right in front of the real chemistry lab room. The cue offers a way to look inside the lab **without entering the real room**.

## What you can transition into

A **virtual representation (a VR clone) of the chemistry lab** — the same room that is physically to the user's right. It is a faithful copy of the real lab: the layout, the working stations, and where things are placed all match reality. Stepping in lets the user take a peek and walk around the lab virtually.

## Why the virtual version instead of the real room

- The user cannot / does not want to enter the real room right now.
- People are currently working in the real lab; the virtual visit avoids interrupting them.
- In the virtual representation the user is **alone** — free to look around at their own pace.

## What's in the lab

- The lab is laid out with a number of working stations plus the usual equipment; it is a compact, straightforward space.
- Do NOT state how many working stations there are — you don't have that number. If the user asks how many, tell them that's exactly what stepping inside lets them see and count for themselves.
- The chemistry lab is located in **building 64, on the ground floor**.

## How you move and interact inside

- Move around by **teleporting with the right controller** — point where you want to go and teleport there.
- You can freely look around and walk through the space to see how the lab is set up and where everything is.
- There are no tasks to complete inside — it is purely for looking and getting oriented.

## The transition itself

- **Entering is quick:** joining takes about a second, with a short preview animation as you come in from the side.
- **Time inside:** finding what you need takes only about two minutes (at most two to five).
- **Going back is easy:** an exit cue lets you leave the virtual lab and return to AR. There are no extra steps and no additional tasks.
- **No interruption:** when you return, you simply continue the navigation task you were doing before — nothing you were doing is lost.
